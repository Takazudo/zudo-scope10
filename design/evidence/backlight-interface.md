# GP13 backlight interface: open-drain stage, voltage table and alternatives (#41, source #19)

Status: **design applied, bench check pending**. G01 and G06 stay OPEN. Everything here is a desk analysis of the carrier netlist (`design/circuit.json`, generated by `scripts/make_design.py`), the Waveshare module schematic and the RP2040 datasheet. No node has been measured; the module revision and population have not been inspected.

## Problem being solved

The module's R16 (10 kΩ) runs from VSYS to `LCD_BL`, which is pin 3 (EN) of CAT1, the backlight regulator (Waveshare Pico-ResTouch-LCD-3.5 schematic p1, "LCD BACKLIGHT", grid 3A; re-read from the PDF at 300 dpi on 2026-09-26, SHA-256 `0547c5fd3dc4fce9e20941b756aa8e348ea47e4d24851e745a04a0c24a5f3b41`, same file as `display-sch` in `g01-display-power.md`). On the carrier VSYS is `+5V_FUSED`, i.e. USB 4.75–5.25 V less the F1 drop. Before #41 the carrier tied GP13 (through R64 33 Ω) directly to `LCD_BL` with R88 100 kΩ to GND, so an undriven GP13 sat near 4.5 V (about 3.85–4.08 V including the RP2040's 50–80 kΩ reset pull-down). That exceeds the RP2040 absolute maximum VPIN ≤ IOVDD + 0.5 V (RP2040 datasheet §5.5.3.1, Table 622) in every state where firmware does not drive the pin. The earlier G01 handoff options, "remove R88" and "accept an estimated < 0.1 mA clamp", are both rejected: removing R88 moves the unloaded node toward 5 V, and the fault-tolerant IO definition does not establish 5 V operation or a usable clamp (#19).

## Decision: N-channel open-drain stage on the carrier

Chosen over the alternatives below because it changes nothing on the module, needs no home soldering, is threshold-agnostic for "off", and keeps the GPIO pad inside its own supply rails by construction rather than by firmware timing.

```text
GP13 (J20-17, net LCD_BL_SRC) --R64 33R-- LCD_BL_GATE --+-- Q1 gate
                                                        |
                                                   R88 100k
                                                        |
                                                       GND
Q1 drain  -- LCD_BL (J30-17) -- module R16 10k -- VSYS ; -- CAT1 EN
Q1 source -- GND
```

- `Q1` is a new generic catalog record `nmos-ll` (logic-level N-channel MOSFET; no MPN, LCSC code or footprint; requirements listed on the record, exact part at G08).
- `R64` keeps its value and its GP13 side; its other side moves from `LCD_BL` to the new net `LCD_BL_GATE`.
- `R88` keeps its value and footprint and becomes the gate pull-down (`LCD_BL_GATE` → GND).
- `J30`/`J31` header nets are unchanged: position 17 is still `LCD_BL`. Module R16 remains the only pull-up on that node.
- `scripts/validate_extra.py` (`_gp13_backlight_interface`) asserts that GP13's net has no resistive DC path below 1 MΩ to `+5V_FUSED`, `VBUS_USB` or `LCD_BL`, that the GP13 and gate nets contain exactly the parts above, and that the display header map is unchanged.

### Default state and enable polarity (explicit)

| GP13 | Q1 | `LCD_BL` | Backlight |
|---|---|---|---|
| undriven (reset, BOOTSEL/ROM, before the firmware hook) | off (gate held at 0 V by R88) | VSYS via R16 | **ON** |
| driven low | off | VSYS via R16 | ON |
| driven high | on | ≈ 0 V | **OFF** |

The backlight is **default ON** and the GPIO control is **active-low** (GP13 high = off). Any future PWM dimming is inverted as well. Firmware is updated accordingly (`firmware/src/lcd_safe_pins.[ch]`, `firmware/src/display_waveshare.c`).

## Voltage/current table

Conventions: IOVDD = 3.3 V nominal (Pico 3V3). "Pad" is the GP13 pad = net `LCD_BL_SRC`; "gate" is net `LCD_BL_GATE`; `LCD_BL` is the module node. V_OH/V_OL are the RP2040 output levels at IOVDD 3.3 V; the RP2040 reset pull-down is the 50–80 kΩ figure cited in #19. The Q1 numbers use the record's requirements (Igss ≤ 1 µA, Rds(on) ≤ 10 Ω at Vgs = 2.5 V); they become datasheet numbers only once a part is selected at G08. **No entry depends on any GPIO protection clamp or on the fault-tolerant IO definition.**

| State | Pad (GP13) | Gate | `LCD_BL` | Current into/out of pad | Backlight |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| USB 4.75 V, GP13 undriven | 0 V (R88 ‖ 50–80 kΩ to GND; Igss ≤ 1 µA × 100 kΩ ≤ 0.1 V worst case) | same as pad | 4.75 V − F1 drop (Q1 off; CAT1 EN input current unknown) | ≤ 1 µA (gate leakage only) | ON |
| USB 5.25 V, GP13 undriven | 0 V (as above) | same as pad | 5.25 V − F1 drop | ≤ 1 µA | ON |
| USB 4.75–5.25 V, GP13 driven low | V_OL ≈ 0 V | ≈ 0 V | VSYS via R16 | ≈ 0 (R88 draws nothing at 0 V) | ON |
| USB 4.75–5.25 V, GP13 driven high | V_OH ≤ IOVDD | V_OH − 33 Ω × (33 µA + Igss) ≈ V_OH | Rds(on) × (VSYS / 10 kΩ) ≤ 10 Ω × 0.53 mA ≈ 5 mV | 33 µA (R88) + Igss, sourced by the pad | OFF |
| Reset (RUN low or power-on reset) | 0 V (reset pull-down 50–80 kΩ plus R88, both to GND) | 0 V | VSYS via R16 | ≤ 1 µA | ON |
| BOOTSEL / boot ROM | expected 0 V (pad left in its reset state); if the ROM ever drove GP13 it could only reach V_OH ≤ IOVDD | follows pad | VSYS via R16 (or ≈ 0 V if driven high) | ≤ 1 µA (or 33 µA) | expected ON; confirm on bench |
| Pico IOVDD not yet up, module VSYS present (power-up ramp; no supported configuration has the module powered with the Pico unpowered, because both take 5 V from the Pico's VBUS pin) | 0 V — the pad is tied only to R88/gate, so VPIN ≤ IOVDD + 0.5 V holds even at IOVDD = 0 | 0 V | VSYS via R16 as soon as VSYS rises | ≈ 0 | ON as soon as CAT1 starts |
| Module absent or its VSYS not present, Pico powered | as in the corresponding driven/undriven row (the pad never depends on the module) | follows pad | floating at Q1's drain (no R16); harmless | ≈ 0 | n/a |

Summary: in every row the pad lies between 0 V and V_OH ≤ IOVDD, so VPIN ≤ IOVDD + 0.5 V and VPIN ≥ −0.5 V are met across USB 4.75–5.25 V, reset, BOOTSEL, both power-sequencing orders and normal operation. The only currents at the pad are the 33 µA R88 load while driven high and the gate leakage; no clamp conducts. Q1's drain sees at most 5.25 V and switches at most ≈ 0.53 mA (VSYS / R16), so a ≥ 20 V, ≥ 10 mA part is ample.

### What the table does not establish

- **CAT1 EN thresholds:** CAT1's part number is not printed on the schematic (text extraction of the PDF gives only "CAT1" with pins VIN/GND/EN/BYP/VOUT), so its V_IH/V_IL are **unknown** (`design/evidence/module-power.json`, `backlight-regulator-en-thresholds`). "Off" relies only on `LCD_BL` being held at a few millivolts, which any enable input treats as low; "on" relies on the module's own R16 arrangement, exactly as when a Pico is stacked on the module. Neither depends on the threshold value.
- **CAT1 EN input current:** unknown; it only affects how close `LCD_BL` sits to VSYS while off, not the pad.
- **The F1 drop and the pre-hook lit interval:** unmeasured.
- **Module revision/population:** R16 = 10 kΩ fitted and CAT1 present are schematic facts, not inspected facts.

## Alternatives weighed

| Option | Default | Why not chosen |
|---|---|---|
| **Factory modification of the module** (remove/replace R16, add a 3.3 V-referenced pull-up) plus a direct GP13 drive | could be OFF | Requires a documented rework of a purchased module by a contracted assembler; every module unit becomes a custom part with a revision-dependent procedure, and the pull-up source on the module (its 3V3 rail) comes up after VSYS through U7. It meets the no-home-soldering rule only if the assembler performs it, and it adds a G01 inspection item per unit. Kept as a fallback if the bench shows CAT1 does not accept the open-drain drive. |
| **Level-translating buffer** (e.g. a 5 V-supplied open-drain buffer or a dedicated translator) | OFF or ON by configuration | Adds a supplied IC and its 5 V decoupling for one signal; its input still has to be protected or supplied from IOVDD; more parts than a MOSFET with no functional gain. |
| **P-channel high-side / default-OFF variants** | OFF | A P-channel device in series with R16's pull-up cannot be inserted without modifying the module (R16 is on the module). A carrier-side default-OFF would need a pull-down strong enough to defeat R16 (a few kΩ, wasting ~1 mA and still a 5 V node) or a supplied logic stage; it also changes the power-state story (backlight current absent before firmware runs). Rejected for P0: default ON matches the vendor's stacked behaviour and the power contract already assumes an unconditional load. |
| **Keep the direct connection, remove R88** | ON | Node approaches 5 V; still violates IOVDD + 0.5 V. Rejected (#19). |
| **Keep the direct connection, accept a clamp current** | ON | Depends on an undocumented clamp; rejected (#19). |

1. Confirm the received module's revision and that R16 (10 kΩ) and CAT1 are populated as drawn; read CAT1's marking if legible and record its EN thresholds from its datasheet, or record them as still unknown.
2. From power-on, capture GP13 (`LCD_BL_SRC`), `LCD_BL_GATE` and `LCD_BL` (J30 position 17) at the lowest and highest USB voltage available within 4.75–5.25 V, through reset (RUN button), BOOTSEL and normal boot of the LCD-off and LCD-on builds. Pass criteria: GP13 and the gate stay within 0 V … IOVDD in every capture; `LCD_BL` sits at VSYS while the backlight is lit and below 50 mV while it is dark; the backlight is dark after the early hook and lit again only after `lcd,init_sequence_sent`.
