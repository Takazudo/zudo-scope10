Sourcing and no-home-soldering contract

Factory scope

Deliver the custom carrier with every fitted electrical item soldered: all passives, six op amps, three muxes, reference/regulator, ten jacks, ten pots, ten range switches, two buttons and all four module connector strips. No “hand-fit after delivery” connector, wire link or programming header is acceptable.

Supply a genuine preheadered Pico H and an assembled Waveshare SKU 19907. Either the assembler integrates them, or the user plugs the tested modules into already-populated sockets while unpowered. Nut tightening, cap fitting, standoffs and plugging a finished harness are mechanical assembly, not soldering. A socketed module does not mean untested electrical assembly is acceptable.

JLCPCB's assembly capabilities describe mixed through-hole and surface-mount routes. Global sourcing and consignment are procurement routes, not approval of this part/board combination. Do not ship supplier parts to an assembler before obtaining receiving and assembly instructions.

REF3330AIDBZR is an exact proposed MPN, but no JLC assembly availability was established. Resistor/capacitor records deliberately include unselected supplier identities rather than invented C-numbers. The Pico sockets, display headers, fuse, knob caps, spacers and mounting screws also need exact mechanical/orderable selection.

Cost honesty

A single display eliminates nine screens and their interconnects, but does not make ten dedicated knobs and range controls free. The selected Waveshare page showed $19.99 for the module on 2026-09-26; it excludes Pico, carrier, controls, assembly, shipping and taxes. NKK switches may dominate the control budget. Obtain the prototype quote before assuming this is a sub-$30 assembled monitor.

For quotation compare three separate figures: populated carrier; preassembled external modules; mechanical finishing/functional test. Separate non-recurring assembly/fixture charges from per-board costs and calculate at the intended prototype quantity. manufacturing/cost-template.json leaves unknown prices null so they cannot disappear into an apparently complete total.

The most promising later cost reductions are qualifying an inexpensive exact SP3T switch, integrating RP2040 onto the board, qualifying a bare non-touch LCD, reducing panel/PCB area, and relaxing dedicated control count only with the user's explicit agreement. P0 does not silently replace the ten controls with a shared menu.
