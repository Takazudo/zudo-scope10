Intended function

Single display with ten panes; touch and SD unused

Recorded findings

480 x 320; portrait logical surface 320 x 480. Active area 48.96 x 73.44 mm in portrait.

5 V module; 3.3 V logic. Vendor module uses a serial-to-16-bit-parallel bridge and RGB565.

Factory female Pico socket headers are present.

Outline 86.00 x 57.20 x 1.6 mm PCB, R1.0 corners; two 1.0 mm recesses on the pin-1 edge (RUN 38.64-47.62, microSD 61.06-76.95 from the header-side edge).

Mounting holes 4.10 from each corner, 77.80 x 49.00 pitch; STEP models dia 5.5 x 4.0 mm standoffs (dia 2.5 bore) on the socket side. Drill diameter not dimensioned.

Female Pico sockets on the non-LCD side: pins 1-20 row 10.45 mm and pins 21-40 row 28.25 mm from the header-side edge (17.80 in STEP, 17.78 nominal); pin 1 and pin 40 at 1.62 mm from the pin-1 edge; pitch 2.54.

Socket housing extends 9.0 mm below the module PCB; modelled cavity depth 8.0 mm. Other underside parts &lt;= 4.0 mm (standoffs), RUN switch 3.5 mm.

Active area 73.44 x 48.96 mm at 3.60 (header side) / 8.96 / 3.97 (pin-1 edge) / 4.27 mm from the board edges; glass top 5.2 mm above module PCB top.
