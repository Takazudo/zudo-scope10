Intended function

Socketed RP2040 module with factory-populated headers and USB

Recorded findings

Factory H version only; the ordinary bare-header Pico does not meet the no-soldering purchase specification.

Pico ADC inputs are GP26-28; GP29 is not a free external ADC pin on this board.

500 kS/s is aggregate. Ten inputs are acquired sequentially.
