The 1 kHz intention is not a guaranteed metrology bandwidth. At 10 ksample/s/channel, Nyquist is 5 kHz; a 20 kHz input can alias severely. Two nominal 3.39 kHz RC poles only provide about 10 dB attenuation at Nyquist. The front end is therefore not a brick-wall anti-alias filter. Do not claim accurate full-audio-band viewing.

Why modules for the first carrier

The Waveshare module documents 480 × 320 pixels, a 5 V supply, an installed female Pico header, a 73.44 × 48.96 mm active area and an 86 × 57.2 mm PCB. Its SPI-to-parallel bridge is part of the module. Buy the exact assembled version.

Pico H includes the headers; an ordinary unheadered Pico does not meet the project requirement. The custom carrier uses two female strips for Pico H and separate male strips for the display. Most spare display contacts are deliberately isolated rather than blindly passed through.

This costs more than a bare RP2040 plus a bare display. The prototype exception avoids designing the MCU's USB/flash/regulator board and an insufficiently documented LCD interface at the same time as ten analog inputs. A later revision may integrate both after their performance is established.

Not selected for P0

HS35B01A / C19632787 remains a cost-down candidate, not the baseline: the public listing does not establish a reviewed serial interface, power arrangement, connector pin map or exact drawing. Do not treat the earlier roughly $9 display figure as the cost of the present module or the finished instrument.

No touchscreen interaction, SD storage, FFT, tuner, signal generator, audio output, battery, network feature or oscillator core is included. TOUCH and SD chip-selects are held inactive. The presence of those parts on the purchased module does not put them in our feature list.
