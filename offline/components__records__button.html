Recorded findings

Vertical/top push; round black actuator; no bracket;1.6N operating force. Tiny switch is not a velocity-sensitive pad or an adequately sized finger key on its own.

Retained XUNPU drawing visually inspected: two SMT contact terminals numbered 1 and 2; normally open. Body 3.9 x 3.0 mm; recommended pad outside span 5.50 mm and inside gap 3.40 mm, pad height 2.00 mm.

Variant code AR02016 = A stainless dome, R no locating post, 020 = H 2.0 mm actuator top above seating plane, 16 = 160 gf.

Exactly two terminals (1 left, 2 right in top view), single NO contact; no internally joined terminal pairs. Terminals 1.30 wide spanning 5.00; actuator dia 1.80; body height 1.50.

Recommended pads 5.50 outside / 3.40 inside / 2.00 high =&gt; 1.05 x 2.00 at +/-2.225; ZudoScope10:SW_XUNPU_TS1088_AR02016_REVIEW matches pad-for-pad (pad 1 at -2.225).
