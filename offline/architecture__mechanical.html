Panel and mechanical study

Concept layout

The 250 × 180 mm panel study puts the portrait screen at the centre, five TIME/RANGE pairs on each side, and the ten input jacks at the outside edges. Left screen column 01–05 matches the left control bank; right column 06–10 matches the right bank. HOLD/LINK sit below the display. Jacks face outward so cables do not lie across the screen.

The SVG has real millimetre dimensions but is an ergonomic study, not a fabrication drawing. The screen active rectangle uses the manufacturer's stated 48.96 × 73.44 mm portrait area. Its exact placement within the module/window and its connector/bushing stack must be verified from the received module/CAD. Socket height, standoff height, underside Pico clearance and USB service access remain open.

mechanical/envelope-study.scad and its exported files describe a nominal slab and control/module envelopes only. They do not include exact terminal geometry or validated mounting holes. They must never be mistaken for a board STEP exported from a routed, populated KiCad design.

Use reference/assets/pot/RK09L1140-F15.STEP for the actual ALPS pot geometry; retain its manufacturer provenance. Do not infer pot rotation/offset relative to a footprint merely because its STEP renders correctly.

Mechanical release checklist

Check shaft length above the final panel, cap and finger spacing, panel thickness, switch lever reach, button access and retention. Use the chosen pot nuts and load-bearing supports rather than leaving all user forces on solder joints. Check plug insertion force, TS plug sleeve/ring behavior, cable bend radius and no live circuitry touching a conductive panel.

Confirm the display and Pico may be unplugged only unpowered and cannot be shifted by one header pin during assembly. Bare unkeyed strip headers do not prevent reversed or offset insertion; specify visible pin-1 markings, mechanical alignment and an inspection procedure or choose keyed connectors for production. No drilling or exact enclosure machining should precede this review.
