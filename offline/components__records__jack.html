Recorded findings

Jack axis parallel to PCB; rear/edge/raised patch strip layout, not Thonkiconn footprint. Two locating posts. Exact manufacturer page1 schematic visually checked: ONLY THREE electrical contact nodes; five legs include duplicated contacts and DO NOT imply normally closed switching contacts. Non-threaded5mm OD nose with3.6mm nominal opening in drawing;3.5mm audiojack class.

Through-hole: land view titled 'PCB hole dimensions (+/-0.1), shown as bottom face'; pin tails 3.2 mm below body.

Five slot openings drawn about 1.5 (along axis) x 1.0 mm; callout reads '(6-1.X1.5)' against 5 drawn (typo). Near row at 1.8 / 5.0 / 8.5 mm from the nose shoulder, far row at 1.8 / 8.5 mm; row pitch 5.8 mm.

Two locating posts dia 1.0 on the part; board holes 2 x dia 1.3 at 2.3 and 8.3 mm from the shoulder, midway between rows. Plating of post holes and slots not stated.

Body 11.5 + 2.5 nose, 6 wide, 5 high; nose OD 5, opening dia 3.6. Contact schematic: 1 = sleeve bar, 2 = V spring, 3 = ^ spring; no normally-closed contact.

3 logical contacts / 5 physical legs: duplicated pad mapping is OPEN (G02 #6).

Coverage and unresolved questions

Footprint independently qualified: NO. Factory order approved: NO. Bench result: NOT TESTED.

  • Verify original PDF view and actual physical terminals before assigning footprint.
  • Factory soldering and mechanical support required; no home electrical rework.
  • STOP: five physical legs represent three contacts; duplicated pads and extra-slot notation are unresolved. No normal switch.
  • Open unconnected jack is biased and must NOT be interpreted as zero volts; shorted-input calibration is required.
  • OPEN (G02 #6): drawing parts table (1 -&gt; terminal 1; contact 2 qty 2 -&gt; terminals 2-3; contact 3 -&gt; 4; PPA base -&gt; 5) cannot be tied to slot positions and is internally inconsistent. A third-party EasyEDA footprint (C668607, contributor Xkcc) numbers near row 1/3/2 and far row 1/2 but its geometry contradicts the manufacturer drawing, so it is not evidence.
