Recorded findings

Manufacturer states SP3T On-On-On, top actuation, straight PC terminals, break-before-make.

Listing showed zero stock; price is not an availability guarantee.

Terminal spacing code D (0.100 in): single row 1-2-3 at 2.54 mm, 3-4 at 5.08 mm; recommended PCB hole (0.8) dia; terminal section (0.4) x (0.6) mm.

Body (15.8) x (4.0) mm, (4.5) mm high; terminals (3.0) mm below body per H48 ((3.4) in H47 isometric; conflict unresolved); actuator (1.2) x (1.5) mm protruding (2.0) mm above body; (2.0) mm travel per step. Actuator top about 6.5 mm above PCB (derived). All reference dims, no tolerances.

Terminal 3 lies on the body and actuator centreline (centre marks, not toleranced). Four corner standoff bosses on the underside; no PCB mounting pegs.

Contact material is silver, not gold: the NKK product page's General Specifications table states "Contact Finish: Silver Plating" (source range-contact-finish).

NKK Electrical Ratings page Z33 (source range-z33) defines Low Level / dry circuits as &lt;=0.4 VA at &lt;=28 V AC or DC max, and recommends gold-plated contacts there because no arc forms to break down tarnish; it recommends silver for Power Level (~100 mA-10 A @125 V AC), stating an arc only develops above about 12 VDC / 0.5 A, which is what keeps a silver contact's oxidation cleaned off. The companion operating-range page (source range-operating-range) restates the same gold-for-dry-circuit recommendation.

Actual sense path (scripts/make_design.py ~47-49): switch COMMON -&gt; 1 k-ohm -&gt; 100 nF -&gt; mux/buffer. For a full 0&lt;-&gt;3.3 V throw: initial capacitor current = 3.3 V / 1 k-ohm = 3.3 mA; RC time constant tau = 1 k-ohm x 100 nF = 100 microseconds. Current then decays to leakage/input-bias current; the centre throw additionally sources through the R40/R41 divider, about 5 k-ohm source impedance. There is no continuous wetting current. The manufacturer's 100 mA / 30 V maximum rating is a ceiling, not evidence that this near-zero-current dry circuit is a suitable application for a silver contact.
